Key insights

  • Churn costs $139K of monthly revenue (30.5% of MRR, ≈$1.67M a year). Churners pay more ($74 vs $61 a month), so revenue churn is higher than customer churn (26.5%).
  • Contract type is the strongest driver: month-to-month customers churn at 43% vs 3% on two-year contracts, and make up 89% of all churners → incentivise annual upgrades.
  • The first 6 months are critical: 53% of new customers leave in that window → onboarding and early check-in calls.
  • Highest-risk segment: month-to-month + fiber optic + electronic check: 1,307 customers with 60% churn ($68K MRR lost). Fiber churn (42%) points to a price/quality issue worth investigating.
  • Support add-ons protect revenue: churn falls from 46% with 1 add-on to 5% with 6. Customers without tech support churn at 42% vs 15% with it → bundle tech support and online security.
  • Targeting model: Logistic Regression (test AUC 0.85). Contacting the top 30% of customers by risk score reaches 67% of churners, 2.2× better than random. 1,026 active customers are flagged high-risk ($79K MRR).
